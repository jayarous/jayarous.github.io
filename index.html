How it works

  1. You tell me what you need

    A message or a short call is enough. No technical words required.

  2. I send a price and a plan

    You know what you are getting, what it costs and when, before any work starts.

  3. You try a working version

    You click through the real thing early and tell me what to change.

  4. It goes live, and I stay around

    I put it online, show you how to use it and fix things that come up.
