What I build

Websites

  • Company and portfolio sites
  • Booking and enquiry pages
  • Small online shops

Apps

An app for the thing you keep doing by hand, built for the phone or the browser and shaped around how you already work.

  • Trackers and planners
  • Customer and member apps
  • Personal tools for you or your family

Software

Custom tools that replace the spreadsheet, the paper form or the copy-and-paste routine your team repeats every week.

  • Quotes, invoices and stock
  • Dashboards and reports
  • Automations between the tools you use

For yourself

A personal site, a hobby project or an idea you have wanted to try. Small budgets and small scopes are welcome.

For your company

How it works

  1. You tell me what you need

    A message or a short call is enough. No technical words required.

  2. I send a price and a plan

    You know what you are getting, what it costs and when, before any work starts.

  3. You try a working version

    You click through the real thing early and tell me what to change.

  4. It goes live, and I stay around

    I put it online, show you how to use it and fix things that come up.
