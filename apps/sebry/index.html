Sebry, a medication reminder app for you and your family

Sebry is an Android app that reminds you to take your medicines and keeps schedules, stock and care routines organised for you or your family. It is built privacy-first: your medicine data is stored on your phone, not on a server.

What Sebry does

Reminders and daily tracking

Medicine reminders and schedules
Create reminders with dose details, timing, food instructions, start and end dates, and repeat options.
Quick dose actions
Mark a reminder as Taken, Skip or Snooze straight from the reminder.
Today view
See today's medicines, upcoming doses, completed doses and what is still to come in one place.
Sound and voice options
Set reminder sounds and ringing behaviour, and use spoken medicine reminders where available.
Quiet notification mode
Temporarily silence reminders or switch them to vibration only.

Medicines, stock and family profiles

Medicine management
Add and edit medicines with name, type, stock quantity, the profile they belong to and optional photos.
Medicine name scan
Scan a medicine label with the camera to help fill in the name. The scan runs on the device and does not need internet.
Family profiles
Create a profile for each person so medicines, schedules, lab reports and care tracking stay organised. Free use includes basic limits; Sebry Plus supports more profiles.
Stock tracking
Track how much of each medicine is left and see when stock is low, so refills are easier to plan.

Health records and Sebry Plus

Lab report tracking
Import lab reports, review the extracted results, filter report history and keep records linked to the right profile. AI-based extraction needs Sebry Plus and your own configured AI provider.
Fluid balance tracking
Log fluid intake and output, view daily totals and export the records. This is part of Sebry Plus.
Optional AI assistance
Sebry Plus users can connect their own AI provider API key for AI chat, medicine summaries, schedule suggestions, AI image scan and lab report extraction. AI features are optional and are off by default.
Backup and restore
Export and import your data as backup files, so you decide when and where it is shared.

Who Sebry is for

  • People managing their own daily medicines
  • Anyone supporting an elderly parent
  • Parents caring for a child's medicines
  • Families keeping several routines in one place

How Sebry handles your data

  • Your medicine data is stored on your device.
  • Sebry does not operate a server for your health data.
  • Sensitive app data is encrypted on the device.
  • Backup files are created only when you choose to export them.
  • AI features are optional and send data only to the provider you configure.
  • Your API key is stored securely on your device.

Good to know

Sebry supports medication routine tracking, reminders and personal record keeping. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow guidance from your doctor, pharmacist or qualified healthcare professional.

Sebry includes adjustable text size and supports English and Malayalam.

Questions about Sebry

Is Sebry free?
Sebry is free to install, and free use includes basic limits such as the number of family profiles. Sebry Plus is a paid upgrade that adds more profiles, fluid balance tracking and the optional AI features.
Does Sebry work without internet?
Yes for the core features. Medicine management, reminders, profiles, history and storage are designed to work offline on your phone, and the medicine name scan also runs on the device.
Where does Sebry store my medicine data?
On your phone. Sebry does not run a server for your health data, sensitive data is encrypted on the device, and backup files exist only if you export them.
Can I manage medicines for my parents or children in Sebry?
Yes. You can create a profile for each person, and each profile keeps its own medicines, schedules, lab reports and care tracking.
Does Sebry give medical advice?
No. Sebry is for reminders, routine tracking and personal records. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
Which languages does Sebry support?
English and Malayalam.
