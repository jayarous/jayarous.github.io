BillGen, an invoicing app for small businesses and freelancers

BillGen is an Android app for creating professional invoices on your phone, sharing them as PDF and keeping track of who has paid. It has tax-ready invoice formats for the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman and India, and it works offline.

What BillGen does

Create invoices quickly

From order to invoice
Build an order from your product list and turn it into an invoice.
Reusable customers and products
Add customers and products once and reuse them every time.
Discounts, taxes and credit notes
Apply discounts, use multiple tax rates and issue credit notes.
PDF sharing and printing
Share or print invoices as PDF.

Tax-ready invoice formats

Who BillGen is for

  • Small businesses that invoice from a phone
  • Freelancers billing regular clients
  • Businesses that issue VAT invoices in the UAE, Saudi Arabia or Oman
  • Businesses that issue GST invoices in India

How BillGen handles your data

  • BillGen works offline, and your data is stored on your phone.
  • You can back up and restore your data.
  • You can protect the app with a fingerprint or screen lock.

Questions about BillGen

Which countries' invoice formats does BillGen support?
The UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman and India, plus a general VAT format. Layouts include tax invoice, simplified invoice, bill of supply and credit note.
Does BillGen work offline?
Yes. BillGen works offline and stores your data on your phone. You can back it up and restore it.
Is BillGen free?
The free plan covers one company and up to 3 new invoices a day. Pro is a subscription that removes the limit and adds features such as recurring invoices, payment follow-ups and your own logo.
Can BillGen share invoices as PDF?
Yes. Invoices can be shared or printed as PDF.
Does BillGen add the QR code needed on Saudi invoices?
Yes. Saudi invoices made in BillGen carry the required QR code. For India, unpaid invoices show a UPI scan-to-pay QR code.
Can BillGen help with a VAT or GST return?
BillGen can export a tax report as CSV to help you prepare your VAT or GST return.
