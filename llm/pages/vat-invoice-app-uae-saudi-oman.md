# How to create a VAT invoice on your phone in the UAE, Saudi Arabia or Oman

BillGen has invoice formats for the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Oman. You choose your country's rules once, and the app checks each invoice for what that country requires before you issue it.

By Jayarous Antony KS, developer of BillGen. Published 17 November 2026.

## Set your country rules

1. In Settings, open Company profile and enter your legal name, address, tax number and currency.
2. Choose the country rules: UAE, Oman or Saudi Arabia.
3. In Settings, open Taxes, add the tax rates you charge and choose the default.
4. On each customer, add their tax number if you want it printed on invoices.

## Create and issue the invoice

1. Tap New order on the Dashboard, pick a customer and add the line items.
2. Create the invoice. With country rules selected, BillGen checks that the invoice has what that country requires before it is issued.
3. Open the invoice and choose View PDF, then share or print it.

BillGen has tax invoice, simplified invoice and credit note layouts.

## Saudi Arabia: the QR code

Simplified tax invoices made in BillGen carry the QR code that ZATCA asks for in its generation phase (phase 1).

BillGen does not support the integration phase (phase 2). Sellers already notified for that phase need an approved solution connected to ZATCA.

## UAE: getting ready for e-invoicing

UAE businesses will send invoices to other businesses as e-invoices through an accredited service provider. You choose and sign up with that provider yourself; BillGen cannot do it for you. Check the UAE Ministry of Finance website for the current dates.

- In Company profile, with the UAE country rules, add your Tax Identification Number, city, emirate and trade licence or Emirates ID.
- On each business customer, switch on Business customer and add their TIN, city and emirate.
- On each product, add an HS code for goods or a SAC code for services.
- Open an invoice to see what is still missing. Only AED invoices are supported for now.

## Tax report

Open Reports to export a tax report as a CSV file, to help prepare your VAT return. BillGen keeps records; it is not tax or financial advice. Check the figures with your accountant before filing.

## Common questions

### Does BillGen send invoices to ZATCA or a tax authority?

No. BillGen creates invoices and PDFs on your phone. It does not submit anything to a tax authority.

### Does BillGen support Saudi e-invoicing phase 2?

No. It adds the phase 1 QR code to simplified tax invoices. The integration phase needs an approved solution connected to ZATCA.

### Can I use BillGen without choosing country rules?

Yes. There is also a general VAT format.

### Does it work offline?

Yes. Your data is stored on your phone, and you can back it up and restore it.
