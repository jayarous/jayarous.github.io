# Sebry, a medicine reminder app that keeps your data on your phone

Sebry is an Android app that reminds you to take your medicines and keeps schedules and stock organised. It works offline and stores your medicine data on your phone, not on a server. The free plan covers one person, three medicines and eight reminders a day.

- Platform: Android
- Price: Free for one profile, three medicines and eight reminders a day. Sebry Plus is a subscription that removes those limits and adds family profiles, backup, reports and the health record tools.
- Category on Google Play: Health & Fitness
- Last updated on Google Play: 2 May 2026
- Made by: Jayarous Antony KS
- Get it: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sebry.app

## What Sebry does

### Reminders and daily tracking

- **Medicine reminders and schedules:** Set a schedule for each medicine: daily, on chosen days or at an interval, as an ongoing medicine, a fixed course or as needed.
- **Alarm-style alerts:** A reminder can show a full-screen alert over the lock screen, speak the medicine name and ring.
- **Quick dose actions:** Answer a reminder with Take, Skip or Snooze, from the notification or the alert screen.
- **Missed-dose follow-ups:** If you do not answer, Sebry reminds you again for up to 30 minutes.
- **Today view:** See today's medicines, upcoming doses, completed doses and what is still to come in one place.
- **Quiet mode:** Silence reminders for a while, or switch them to vibration only.

### Medicines and stock

- **Medicine details:** Save each medicine with its name, strength, form, expiry date, stock and optional photos.
- **Medicine name scan:** Photograph a medicine label and Sebry suggests the name. The scan runs on the phone and does not need internet.
- **Stock tracking:** Each dose you mark as taken is subtracted from the stock. Sebry warns you inside the app when about 7, 3 and 1 days of a medicine are left.
- **Expiry labels:** Medicines that have expired or expire soon are labelled.

### With Sebry Plus

- **No limits and family profiles:** More than three medicines, more than eight reminders a day, and a profile for each family member.
- **Backup and restore:** Export your data as a backup file and import it again. Lab reports and photo files are not part of the backup.
- **Dose analytics and PDF reports:** See adherence over 7 to 90 days and share a PDF report.
- **Lab reports:** Import a lab report as a photo or PDF and review the extracted results. This also needs your own AI provider key.
- **Fluid balance:** Log fluid intake and output, view daily totals and export the records as CSV.
- **Optional AI tools:** AI chat, medicine summaries, schedule suggestions and photo scan, using your own AI provider key. They are off until you set them up.

## Who Sebry is for

- People managing their own daily medicines
- Anyone supporting an elderly parent
- Parents caring for a child's medicines
- Families keeping several routines in one place

## How Sebry handles your data

- Your medicine data is stored on your device.
- Sebry does not operate a server for your health data.
- Medicines, schedules and profiles are stored encrypted on the device.
- There is no account and no sign-in.
- Backup files are created only when you choose to export them. A backup file itself is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
- AI features are optional and send data only to the provider you configure.
- Your API key is stored securely on your device.

## Good to know

Sebry supports medication routine tracking, reminders and personal record keeping. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow guidance from your doctor, pharmacist or qualified healthcare professional.

Sebry has an adjustable text size. The app is in English, with Malayalam available for the main screens. Notifications and spoken reminders are in English.

## Questions about Sebry

### Is Sebry free?

Yes, for one profile, three medicines and eight reminders a day, with no ads. Sebry Plus is a subscription that removes those limits and adds family profiles, backup, reports, lab reports, fluid balance and the optional AI tools.

### Does Sebry work without internet?

Yes. Medicines, schedules, reminders and stock work offline, and the medicine name scan runs on the phone. Only the optional AI tools need internet.

### Where does Sebry store my medicine data?

On your phone. Sebry has no account and does not run a server for your health data. Medicines, schedules and profiles are stored encrypted on the device.

### Can I manage medicines for my parents or children in Sebry?

Yes, with Sebry Plus. The free plan has one profile. With Plus you can create a profile for each person, and each profile keeps its own medicines and schedules.

### Does Sebry warn me when a medicine is running out?

Yes. It shows a warning inside the app when about 7, 3 and 1 days of a medicine are left, based on your schedules. It does not send a push notification for low stock.

### Does Sebry give medical advice?

No. Sebry is for reminders, routine tracking and personal records. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

### Which languages does Sebry support?

English, with Malayalam for the main screens. Notifications and spoken reminders are in English.
