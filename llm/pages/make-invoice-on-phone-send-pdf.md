# How to make an invoice on your phone and send it as a PDF

You can create an invoice on an Android phone, turn it into a PDF and send it to your customer by WhatsApp or email. This guide uses BillGen, an invoice maker that works offline and keeps your data on your phone.

By Jayarous Antony KS, developer of BillGen. Published 17 November 2026.

## What you need

- Your business name and address
- Your customer's name, plus a phone number or email if you want to send the invoice straight from the app
- The items or services you are billing, with prices
- The tax rate, if you charge tax

## Set up once

1. Install BillGen from Google Play and open it. It starts with a Demo Company so you can look around.
2. Tap "Set up my business" on the Dashboard banner and enter your company name. The sample data is removed automatically.
3. In Settings, open Company profile and add your legal name, address, tax number, currency and country rules.
4. In Settings, open Taxes, add the tax rates you charge and choose the default.
5. Open Customers and tap Add. Only the name is required.
6. Open Products and tap Add. A name and a price are required. You can also import a product list from a CSV file.

## Create the invoice

In BillGen you create an order first, then turn it into an invoice.

1. Tap New order on the Dashboard, pick a customer and add at least one line item.
2. Choose payment terms to give the invoice a due date.
3. Create the invoice. It gets the next number in your sequence.

## Send it

1. Open the invoice and choose View PDF to preview it.
2. Share or print the PDF from the preview.
3. To send it straight to the customer, use Send via WhatsApp or Send via email on the invoice. These use the phone number or email saved on the customer.

## Good to know

- The free plan covers one company and up to 3 new invoices a day. Pro is a subscription that removes the limit.
- Billing the same customer again? Open the earlier invoice and choose Duplicate invoice.
- Editing a customer later does not change invoices you have already issued.
- Your data lives on your phone only, so back it up from Settings.

## Common questions

### Can I make an invoice without internet?

Yes. BillGen works offline and stores your data on your phone. You need a connection only to send the PDF by WhatsApp or email.

### Is BillGen free?

The free plan covers one company and up to 3 new invoices a day. Pro is a subscription that removes the limit and adds features such as recurring invoices, payment follow-ups and your own logo.

### Can I put my logo on the invoice?

A company logo is a Pro feature. The header and footer text come from Settings, under Branding.

### How do I record that a customer has paid?

Open the invoice and choose Record payment. Enter the amount, date and method. A part payment marks the invoice Partially paid, and a full payment marks it Paid.
