# How to message each other on a cruise without Wi-Fi

At sea your phone has no normal mobile coverage, and the ship's Wi-Fi is usually a paid extra. Android phones that are near each other can still exchange messages directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, using an offline chat app installed before you sail.

By Jayarous Antony KS, developer of AirGrid. Published 17 November 2026.

## Why your usual apps stop working

Messaging apps need an internet connection. Away from the coast there are no mobile towers, so they only work through the ship's Wi-Fi or a maritime roaming service, and both usually cost extra. Check the prices with your cruise line and your mobile operator before you travel.

## What works without internet

An offline chat app connects phones to each other directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct. Nothing leaves the phones, so it works at sea in the same way it works on land. AirGrid is an Android app that does this, and it can pass messages from phone to phone when several people nearby are using it.

## Before you board

1. Install AirGrid on every Android phone in your group while you still have internet.
2. Open it on each phone and allow the permissions it asks for.
3. Try it at home with the phones side by side, so everyone knows how it works.
4. On board, if you want to avoid roaming charges, switch on airplane mode and then turn Bluetooth and Wi-Fi back on.
5. Open AirGrid and use private chat for your own group.

## Limits on a ship

- Range is short. Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct reach people nearby, not across a whole ship, and walls and decks reduce it further.
- Messages can hop between phones running AirGrid, so reach improves when more people around you use it.
- It connects Android phones that have the app installed.
- Agree a meeting place and time as a backup.

## Common questions

### Does Bluetooth chat work at sea?

Yes. The phones connect to each other directly, so no mobile network or ship Wi-Fi is needed.

### Will it reach my family in another part of the ship?

Probably not directly. Range is short and depends on the phones and the surroundings. It works best when you are in the same area, such as the same restaurant, lounge or pool area.

### Will I be charged for using it?

AirGrid does not use internet or mobile data. To avoid roaming charges from other apps, keep mobile data off, or use airplane mode with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned back on.
