## Staying in touch without internet

These guides use AirGrid, an offline chat app for Android. https://jayarous.github.io/apps/airgrid/

- **How to chat without internet on Android:** Two Android phones can exchange messages with no internet and no mobile data, as long as they are near each other and run the same offline chat app. The phones connect directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct instead of going through a mobile network or a router. https://jayarous.github.io/guides/chat-without-internet-android/
- **How to message each other on a cruise without Wi-Fi:** At sea your phone has no normal mobile coverage, and the ship's Wi-Fi is usually a paid extra. Android phones that are near each other can still exchange messages directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, using an offline chat app installed before you sail. https://jayarous.github.io/guides/message-on-cruise-without-wifi/
- **How to message someone on the same flight without Wi-Fi:** If your group is seated apart on a flight, Android phones can message each other directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct. Nothing goes through the internet, so it works without in-flight Wi-Fi. https://jayarous.github.io/guides/message-on-flight-without-wifi/
- **A walkie talkie app that works without internet:** Many walkie talkie apps send your voice over the internet, so they stop when the signal does. AirGrid sends voice directly between nearby Android phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, so it keeps working with no internet or mobile data. https://jayarous.github.io/guides/walkie-talkie-app-without-internet/
- **How to talk to another rider without mobile signal:** On rides through hills and remote roads the mobile signal comes and goes. AirGrid's Rider Mode gives two Android phones a live audio link over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, with no internet or mobile data. https://jayarous.github.io/guides/talk-to-another-rider-without-mobile-signal/

## Invoicing on your phone

These guides use BillGen, an invoice maker for Android. https://jayarous.github.io/apps/billgen/

- **How to make an invoice on your phone and send it as a PDF:** You can create an invoice on an Android phone, turn it into a PDF and send it to your customer by WhatsApp or email. This guide uses BillGen, an invoice maker that works offline and keeps your data on your phone. https://jayarous.github.io/guides/make-invoice-on-phone-send-pdf/
- **How to create a VAT invoice in the UAE, Saudi Arabia or Oman:** BillGen has invoice formats for the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Oman. You choose your country's rules once, and the app checks each invoice for what that country requires before you issue it. https://jayarous.github.io/guides/vat-invoice-app-uae-saudi-oman/
- **How to create a GST invoice with a UPI QR code:** BillGen has an India invoice format. It splits tax into CGST and SGST or charges IGST based on the customer's state, prints HSN and SAC codes, and puts a UPI scan-to-pay QR code on unpaid invoices. https://jayarous.github.io/guides/gst-invoice-app-india-upi-qr/
