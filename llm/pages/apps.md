# Android apps by Jayarous Antony KS

Jayarous Antony KS builds and publishes Android apps on Google Play. Each app below has its own page with what it does, what it costs and answers to common questions.

- **Sebry** (Health & Fitness): Medication reminders, schedules and family health profiles in one app. Your data stays on your phone. Page: https://jayarous.github.io/apps/sebry/ Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sebry.app
- **BillGen** (Business): Invoicing for small businesses and freelancers: GST and VAT invoices, payment tracking and PDF sharing. Works offline. Page: https://jayarous.github.io/apps/billgen/ Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.billgen.app
- **Game Mode Pro** (Tools): A floating HUD with estimated FPS and alerts for heat and low battery, plus touch lock, screen recording and refresh rate profiles. Page: https://jayarous.github.io/apps/game-mode-pro/ Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gamemodepro.app
- **AirGrid** (Communication): Chat with nearby Android phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, with no internet or mobile data. Page: https://jayarous.github.io/apps/airgrid/ Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.airgrid.app

All four apps are published under the developer name Jayarous: https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=Jayarous
