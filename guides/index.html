Short how-to guides by Jayarous Antony KS for the Android apps he builds.

Staying in touch without internet

These guides use AirGrid, an offline chat app for Android. About AirGrid

  • How to chat without internet on Android

    Two Android phones can exchange messages with no internet and no mobile data, as long as they are near each other and run the same offline chat app. The phones connect directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct instead of going through a mobile network or a router.

  • How to message each other on a cruise without Wi-Fi

    At sea your phone has no normal mobile coverage, and the ship's Wi-Fi is usually a paid extra. Android phones that are near each other can still exchange messages directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, using an offline chat app installed before you sail.

  • How to message someone on the same flight without Wi-Fi

    If your group is seated apart on a flight, Android phones can message each other directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct. Nothing goes through the internet, so it works without in-flight Wi-Fi.

  • A walkie talkie app that works without internet

    Many walkie talkie apps send your voice over the internet, so they stop when the signal does. AirGrid sends voice directly between nearby Android phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, so it keeps working with no internet or mobile data.

  • How to talk to another rider without mobile signal

    On rides through hills and remote roads the mobile signal comes and goes. AirGrid's Rider Mode gives two Android phones a live audio link over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, with no internet or mobile data.

Invoicing on your phone

These guides use BillGen, an invoice maker for Android. About BillGen

  • How to make an invoice on your phone and send it as a PDF

    You can create an invoice on an Android phone, turn it into a PDF and send it to your customer by WhatsApp or email. This guide uses BillGen, an invoice maker that works offline and keeps your data on your phone.

  • How to create a VAT invoice in the UAE, Saudi Arabia or Oman

    BillGen has invoice formats for the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Oman. You choose your country's rules once, and the app checks each invoice for what that country requires before you issue it.

  • How to create a GST invoice with a UPI QR code

    BillGen has an India invoice format. It splits tax into CGST and SGST or charges IGST based on the customer's state, prints HSN and SAC codes, and puts a UPI scan-to-pay QR code on unpaid invoices.

Gaming on Android

These guides use Game Mode Pro, a gaming HUD for Android. About Game Mode Pro

  • How to show a HUD overlay over your games

    Game Mode Pro floats a small bubble over your game that opens into a HUD. It shows estimated FPS and warns you when the phone runs hot or the battery runs low.

  • How to set 120 Hz for games without root

    Game Mode Pro can switch your screen to its highest refresh rate when a game session starts and put your own setting back when it ends. It uses the free Shizuku app instead of root, and not every phone allows it.

  • How to stop accidental touches and notifications while gaming

    Game Mode Pro puts a touch lock and a Do Not Disturb switch in a floating HUD, so you can block stray touches and silence notifications without leaving your game.

Taking medicines on time

These guides use Sebry, a medicine reminder app for Android. Sebry does not provide medical advice. About Sebry

  • How to set a medicine reminder on Android

    Sebry reminds you when a medicine is due and lets you answer with Take, Skip or Snooze. It works offline, needs no account, and is free for up to three medicines.

  • Medicine reminders not ringing: what to check

    Android can delay or silence reminders from any app to save battery. These are the settings that decide whether a medicine reminder arrives on time, and where Sebry shows them.

  • How to track medicine stock and know when to refill

    Sebry counts down your stock each time you mark a dose as taken, and warns you inside the app when about a week of a medicine is left.
