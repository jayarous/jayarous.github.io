How to message someone on the same flight without Wi-Fi

If your group is seated apart on a flight, Android phones can message each other directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct. Nothing goes through the internet, so it works without in-flight Wi-Fi.

By Jayarous Antony KS, developer of AirGrid. Published 17 November 2026.

Is it allowed?

Airplane mode switches off the mobile connection. On Android you can turn Bluetooth and Wi-Fi back on while airplane mode stays on. Many airlines allow Bluetooth during the flight, but rules differ, so check your airline's policy and always follow the crew's instructions.

Before you fly

  1. Install AirGrid on each Android phone while you have internet.
  2. Open it and allow the permissions it asks for.
  3. Test it together before you leave for the airport.
  4. On the plane, switch on airplane mode, then turn Bluetooth and Wi-Fi back on.
  5. Open AirGrid and use private chat to message the people you are travelling with.

Limits to know

  • Range is short and varies with the phones and the surroundings, so it may not reach from one end of a large aircraft to the other.
  • Public nearby chat reaches everyone connected to the same local mesh. Use private chat for your own group.
  • It connects Android phones that have the app installed.

Common questions

Can I use Bluetooth in airplane mode?
On Android, yes: switch on airplane mode, then turn Bluetooth back on. Whether you may use it during the flight depends on the airline, so check its policy.
Does it need in-flight Wi-Fi?
No. Messages go directly from phone to phone, so no in-flight Wi-Fi or internet is used.
Can I message someone who has an iPhone?
AirGrid is an Android app, so it connects Android phones that have it installed.
