How to track your medicine stock and know when to refill

Sebry counts down your stock each time you mark a dose as taken, and warns you inside the app when about a week of a medicine is left.

By Jayarous Antony KS, developer of Sebry. Published 17 November 2026.

Set it up

  1. Open the Medicines tab and add a medicine, or open one you already have.
  2. Enter the Stock: the number you have, and the unit.
  3. Make sure the medicine has a schedule with a Dose. Sebry uses the schedule to work out how many days of stock are left.

How the count works

Each time you answer a reminder with Take, Sebry subtracts that dose from the stock. Skip does not change it. If the stock is lower than the dose, Sebry does not record the dose and shows "Not enough stock to log this dose."

When you buy more, edit the medicine and update the Stock number.

Low-stock warnings

Sebry shows a warning when about 7, 3 and 1 days of a medicine are left, once at each point. The warnings appear inside the app, not as push notifications, so you see them when you open Sebry. The 7, 3 and 1 day points cannot be changed.

With Sebry Plus, the warning has a Notify button that opens your phone's share menu with a short refill message you can send to someone.

Expiry dates

If you enter an Expiry date, Sebry labels the medicine as Expiring soon or Expired. It does not send a notification about expiry.

Common questions

Is stock tracking free?
Yes. Stock tracking and the low-stock warnings are in the free plan. The Notify button is part of Sebry Plus.
Does Sebry order medicines or contact a pharmacy?
No. Sebry keeps your own records on your phone. It does not connect to any pharmacy or healthcare service.
Can I track a medicine I only take when needed?
Yes. Give it an As needed schedule. No reminders are set, and you can still record doses and track stock.
Does Sebry notify me when stock is low?
It shows a warning inside the app. It does not send a push notification for low stock.
