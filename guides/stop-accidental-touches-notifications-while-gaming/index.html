How to stop accidental touches and notifications while gaming on Android

Game Mode Pro puts a touch lock and a Do Not Disturb switch in a floating HUD, so you can block stray touches and silence notifications without leaving your game.

By Jayarous Antony KS, developer of Game Mode Pro. Published 17 November 2026.

Lock the screen against touches

  1. In Game Mode Pro, open the Tools tab and turn on Overlay HUD, then open your game.
  2. Open the HUD from the bubble and tap Lock.
  3. The screen now ignores touches. To unlock it, hold the lock icon for about a second and a half.

Touch lock helps when a game plays by itself for a while, or during a cutscene or a loading screen.

Silence notifications

  1. Grant Do Not Disturb access. Game Mode Pro asks for it on the setup screen, and the Tools tab shows a Grant DND button until you do.
  2. Tap DND in the HUD, or in Quick Controls on the Tools tab.

DND switches Android's Do Not Disturb to priority only, so only the interruptions you allow in Android's Do Not Disturb settings come through. It is a switch, not a timer. Tap DND again to turn it off when you finish playing.

Set the brightness

Quick Controls also has a brightness slider. It needs the Modify system settings access. Moving the slider turns adaptive brightness off, and the Auto toggle turns it back on.

Screenshots and recording

Tap Shot in the HUD to save a screenshot to the Game Mode Pro folder in Pictures. The bubble hides itself while the picture is taken. Recordings are saved to the Game Mode Pro folder in Movies at 720p, 1080p or native resolution, as video only with no sound. Android asks for permission each time you capture the screen. Starting a recording from the HUD is part of Pro.

Common questions

Are touch lock and Do Not Disturb free?
Yes. Touch lock, the Do Not Disturb switch, the brightness slider and screenshots are in the free plan.
Does Game Mode Pro turn Do Not Disturb off when I stop playing?
No. You turn it off yourself with the same DND switch.
How do I unlock the screen after a touch lock?
Hold the lock icon for about a second and a half.
Do recordings include sound?
No. Recordings are video only.
