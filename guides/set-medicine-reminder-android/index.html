How to set a medicine reminder on your Android phone

Sebry reminds you when a medicine is due and lets you answer with Take, Skip or Snooze. It works offline, needs no account, and is free for up to three medicines.

By Jayarous Antony KS, developer of Sebry. Published 17 November 2026.

Add the medicine

  1. Install Sebry from Google Play and open it. On first launch it walks you through two steps: add a medicine, then create a schedule.
  2. Enter the Medication name. You can also photograph the label and Sebry suggests the name.
  3. Add the Strength and choose the form, such as tablet, capsule or syrup.
  4. Enter the Stock you have, if you want Sebry to count it down.
  5. Save the medicine.

Create the schedule

  1. Set the Dose and its unit, as your doctor or pharmacist told you.
  2. Choose the Course Type: Ongoing, Fixed course or As needed. As needed sets no reminders.
  3. Add the times under Time.
  4. Choose the Frequency: daily, chosen days of the week, or an interval.
  5. For a fixed course, set the Start Date and End Date.
  6. Save the schedule.

When a reminder arrives

Sebry shows a notification with Take, Skip and Snooze. Take records the dose and subtracts it from your stock. Snooze repeats the reminder after 5 minutes. If you do not answer, Sebry reminds you again for up to 30 minutes.

Out of the box, Sebry speaks the reminder instead of ringing. If you want a ringtone, open Settings, then Notifications, and turn off Play TTS only.

Let Android deliver it on time

Android can delay reminders from any app to save battery. Sebry's Home screen shows a button for each setting that is missing: Enable notifications, Enable exact alarms and Disable battery optimization. Tap each one and allow it.

Common questions

Is Sebry free?
Yes, for one profile, three medicines and eight reminders a day. Sebry Plus is a subscription that removes those limits.
Does Sebry need internet or an account?
No. Reminders work offline, and there is no account or sign-in.
Does Sebry tell me what dose to take?
No. You enter the dose your doctor or pharmacist gave you. Sebry does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
Can I change how long Snooze waits?
On the free plan Snooze is 5 minutes. With Sebry Plus you can set it from 1 to 30 minutes.
