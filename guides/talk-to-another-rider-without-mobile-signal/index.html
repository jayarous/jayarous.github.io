How to talk to another rider without mobile signal

On rides through hills and remote roads the mobile signal comes and goes. AirGrid's Rider Mode gives two Android phones a live audio link over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, with no internet or mobile data.

By Jayarous Antony KS, developer of AirGrid. Published 17 November 2026.

What Rider Mode is

Rider Mode is a trusted one-to-one live audio session. It has mute controls and a voice-activated microphone, so you do not need to press a button to talk. It is built for close-range coordination between two people.

Set it up before the ride

  1. Install AirGrid on both Android phones while you have internet.
  2. Meet up and open AirGrid on both phones so they discover each other.
  3. Mark each other as trusted contacts.
  4. Start a Rider Mode session.
  5. Test it with your helmet and headset before you set off, because audio setups differ.

Limits and safety

  • Rider Mode is one-to-one, not a group channel.
  • It works at close range. Performance varies with the phones, the Android version, distance and radio conditions, so expect it to drop when you ride far apart.
  • Set everything up while stopped. Do not handle your phone while riding, and follow your local road rules.

Common questions

Does Rider Mode need internet?
No. The audio goes directly between the two phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct.
Can more than two riders join?
Rider Mode is one-to-one. For a group, short walkie clips can be sent to everyone on the local mesh.
Will it work with my helmet headset?
It depends on your phone and headset. Try it before the ride.
